## Exact conclusion from the import audit

1. **The native finite dictionary is real and verified.** In particular there are algebraic Möbius/convolution, masked selected-subset and full physical first-owner cross-term identities; genuine finite sixth-root residues and x/R 317/1027 checks. These should be REUSED, not reimplemented or relabelled.
2. **The two frameworks are NOT the same analytical input yet.** OAI's \(u,P,\eta\) lives in a family of Eisenstein ideal characters with actual conductor and prime-slot height; the native \(w_R(pN(\alpha))\) is a moving sharp owner weight with extra clipping and historical descendants. The rank-three counterexample at R=317 and exact same-norm/different-phase theorem forbid a termwise norm-only or two-rectangle projection.
3. **The main unsupported inference is from native exact finite source equalities to OAI's UNIFORM analytic estimates.** To complete a *genuinely independent* native 7/8 proof, derive and prove on the original fully tagged native source the exact genuine ideal-to-owner harmonic source equation (normalizers, moving weights, excluded primes and actual smooth-to-sharp errors), all nonprincipal inverse second/plain fourth/mixed height moments, and the low/reflected analytic detector. Proving them in the new carrier is new mathematics; citing an OAI theorem proves the external source result, not the new statement on the native carrier.
4. **The 317/1027 witnesses verify the phase **coefficient** and that it cannot factor via scalar integer norm. They do not establish a universal positive-moment or Fourier analytic inequality.** Finite checks are admissible regression tests for a proposed formula; they are never substitutes for the all-conductor/all-height estimate.
5. **No \`sorry\`, axioms or OAI imports are to be inserted to force a headline theorem.** The current PR remains draft until a single end-to-end RH_Lean derivation yields the terminal theorem with only standard Lean axioms.

The natural completed proof architecture is:
\[

\to \text{source-faithful compensated theta/Poisson rows}
\to \text{uniform inverse second + plain fourth moments}
