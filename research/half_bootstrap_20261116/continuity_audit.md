## 3. Independent falsification: central-row improvement alone

The exact live hypotheses of [original_normalized_compensatedPhysicalProbe_low_total_mass](https://github.com/OVVO-Financial/RH_Lean/blob/076195b75278869d4f18fd924b9ebdaedf723108/research/oai_434/VF919TotalMassLowPhysical.lean) include total slot length 1/6 and

    (3/2)(5/3−2(x+y)) ≤ 1/6.

Hence x+y≥7/9. Its normalized LOW exponent is (5x+y)/12 plus an arbitrary positive loss. The [source_scale_identity_variable_xy and source_power_identity_variable_x](https://github.com/OVVO-Financial/RH_Lean/blob/076195b75278869d4f18fd924b9ebdaedf723108/research/oai_434/VF919FiniteSlotReflectedGain.lean) fix the principal shift c=x/3−29/36. Subtraction yields

    b_LOW = 29/36+(x+y)/12 ≥ 47/54 = 0.8703703703…
    47/54−1/2 = 10/27.

This is exact rational algebra. It does not depend on the central-row exponent. Even an ideal central estimate cannot make this unchanged LOW supplier certify b≤3/4, much less b arbitrarily close to 1/2. More precise central estimates can improve a nearby finite endpoint; they do not alone establish a half-bootstrap.

**Scope:** this is a barrier to the displayed componentwise certification architecture. It is not a lower bound for the true source, a universal limitation on all LOW theorems, or a proof that a joint signed estimate cannot bypass componentwise losses. In particular, the attached handoff’s “only a smaller central row count can” wording should not be generalized to every possible signed complete-source method.

## 4. Independent falsification: repeating the Y-tilt

Write the cumulative Y-only movement as

    x=17/48, y=23/48−12Γ.

Then x+y=5/6−12Γ, so the same LOW condition is Γ≤1/216. The nominal boundary is 7/8−Γ, and its minimum under this supplier is exactly 47/54.

Splitting Γ into many small tilts does not alter this identity. If one repeatedly subtracts a certified initial gain while resetting the original LOW margin, one has changed the hypotheses between stages. The missing theorem is a new boundary-dependent supplier or a source-faithful transition to another mechanism. It is not another arithmetic evaluation of the same initial margin.

This also explains why formalizing the initial finite improvement, including a better Y-tilt, cannot itself certify an iteration. It may still be reusable support if a real continuation consumes it.

## 6. Independent falsification: a fixed allowance is not a half-bootstrap

The already-compiled [AmplificationExponentIteration.lean](https://github.com/OVVO-Financial/RH_Lean/blob/076195b75278869d4f18fd924b9ebdaedf723108/RHLean/Proof/AmplificationExponentIteration.lean), blob d78be4509e5dcc35b833c3786258b94fc6e71915, proves

    e_next=(e+eta)/2, where e=b−1/2.

For fixed eta, iteration gives e_n=eta+2^(−n)(e_0−eta), hence b_n→1/2+eta. Initial strict improvement eta<e_0 does not suffice. A valid renewable eta(b)≤rho e, with one rho<1, gives geometric convergence to zero. More general gain laws are possible, but their convergence and admissible renewal must be proved.

The file’s all_exponents_of_contracting_upgrade explicitly assumes the arithmetic upgrade for every b>1/2. It says that the exact Möbius square-endpoint and strict-history supplier is outstanding. That assumption cannot be counted as a theorem supplied by compiling the consumer.

Likewise, “strictly better at every stage” alone does not exclude a positive limiting gap: the explicit sequence b_n=3/4+(b_0−3/4)2^(−n) is strictly decreasing but converges to 3/4. A continuation record must settle the limit, not only positivity of individual decrements.

The correct endpoint quantifier is every b>1/2 (or an equivalent RH consumer). It does not require a uniform O(sqrt(x)) constant or uniform constants across infinitely many stages. For a fixed target b, a finite sequence of stage-dependent constants suffices if each constant is independent of x.
