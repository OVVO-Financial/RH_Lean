#### DAMP1: Exact source, compatible centering and inverse

All arithmetic functions below are on positive integers; convolution is Dirichlet convolution; `eps` is its unit and `one(n)=1`. All sums include both endpoints. Let Lambda be the genuine von Mangoldt function, including every prime power, and

    e(N)=psi(N)-N,
    b_gamma(n)=Lambda(n)-1+2 gamma eps(n),
    w_alpha(n)=n^(-alpha),
    mu_alpha(n)=mu(n)n^(-alpha),
    kappa_alpha=one*mu_alpha,
    beta_alpha=mu*w_alpha.

For alpha>0, kappa_alpha(n)=product_{p|n}(1-p^(-alpha))>0, including kappa_alpha(1)=1. Thus this is not a bounded-depth truncation. The standard multiplicative divisor product is recorded in [NIST DLMF 27.6.2](https://dlmf.nist.gov/27.6#E2); here it is applied to the completely multiplicative function n^(-alpha), with the empty product explicitly restored.

Since mu*one=eps and mu_alpha*w_alpha=eps,

    beta_alpha*kappa_alpha=eps,
    beta_alpha(p^j)=p^(-alpha(j-1))(p^(-alpha)-1), j>=1.

In particular beta has nonzero prime-power terms, not squarefree-only support.

The elementary identity one*Lambda=log gives

    a_gamma=one*b_gamma=log(n)-tau(n)+2 gamma,
    A_gamma(m)=sum_{n<=m}a_gamma(n)
             =log(m!)-sum_{n<=m}tau(n)+2 gamma m.

For every real alpha>=0 and integer N>=1, finite convolution and floor associativity give the exact equalities

    U_alpha(N)=sum_{d<=N}kappa_alpha(d)(e(floor(N/d))+2 gamma)
              =sum_{d<=N}mu(d)d^(-alpha) A_gamma(floor(N/d)),
    e(N)+2 gamma=sum_{d<=N}beta_alpha(d) U_alpha(floor(N/d)).

Proof: the coefficient function of U is kappa_alpha*b_gamma=mu_alpha*a_gamma. Summing its coefficients gives the two floor sums; convolving with beta gives b_gamma. Every ordered divisor occurrence, d=1, the last floor cell, and every prime power is retained.

This source matches `nativePNTError N := nativePsi N - N` in [NativePNTErrorMass.lean](https://github.com/OVVO-Financial/RH_Lean/blob/13b31c6313e274a073d82a01175201185e59e37e/RHLean/Analysis/NativePNTErrorMass.lean), blob `6779156e0fd49eee0c04d7a05825d6af8d5abed0`. `nativePsi` is the sum of Lambda over `Finset.Icc 1 N`; the exact prime-power decomposition was also inspected in NativePNTChebyshev, blob `f88e3ae9da124aa23ad160ab1cf5389376486948`.

    U_alpha(N)=T_alpha(N)+2 gamma sum_{d<=N}kappa_alpha(d).

Replacing the last sum by an asymptotic linear surrogate would leave an unproved return. I do not do that. At alpha=0, kappa_0=beta_0=eps and U_0=e+2 gamma: there is no smoothing. #### DAMP2: An independent uniform square-root bound for the damped object

There is one absolute constant C0 such that for every integer m>=1,

    |A_gamma(m)| <= C0 sqrt(m).

Here is a complete elementary argument. Put k=floor(sqrt(m)). The hyperbola identity gives

    sum_{n<=m}tau(n)=2 sum_{d<=k}floor(m/d)-k^2
                   =2m H_k-k^2+O(k).

The harmonic estimate H_k=log k+gamma+O(1/k), and k^2<=m<(k+1)^2, give m log m+(2 gamma-1)m+O(sqrt(m)). Constants are absolute, valid after enlarging them to include m=1. Monotonicity of log also gives

    m log m-m+1 <= log(m!)
                       <= m log m-m+1+log m.

Subtracting cancels both main terms and proves the displayed bound. The divisor formula is independently documented for all real x>=1 in [NIST DLMF 27.11.2](https://dlmf.nist.gov/27.11#E2); Stirling's expansion is an alternative confirmation in [NIST DLMF 5.11.1](https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.11#E1). Only positive real/integer arguments are used, with no height or conductor transfer. The proof above needs neither PNT nor a Mobius cancellation estimate.

Consequently, for every alpha>=1/2 and integer N>=1,

    |U_alpha(N)| <= C0 sqrt(N) sum_{d<=N}d^(-alpha-1/2)
                 <= C0 sqrt(N)(1+log N).

For each fixed alpha>1/2 the sharper constant C0*zeta(alpha+1/2) gives |U_alpha(N)|<=C_alpha sqrt(N). At alpha=1/2 the stated logarithm is retained. The universal C0 precedes both alpha and N. This is a genuine independent estimate on the transformed object, not a proof of the desired estimate on e. Status: PAPER_PROOF / SELF_AUDIT.

#### DAMP3: Linear absolute recovery on the actual arithmetic source

For fixed alpha>0 set f_alpha=|beta_alpha| and g_alpha=mu*f_alpha. These are multiplicative. For q=p^(-alpha),

    f_alpha(p^j)=(1-q)q^(j-1), j>=1,

    g_alpha(p^j)=-(1-q)^2 q^(j-2), j>=2.

Hence

    sum_{j>=1}|g_alpha(p^j)|/p^j
       =q/p+(1-q)^2/(p^2(1-q/p)).

The sum of these local quantities over primes converges for every fixed alpha>0, by comparison with sum_{n>=2}(n^(-1-alpha)+2n^(-2)). Therefore sum_n |g_alpha(n)|/n<infinity. Since f_alpha=one*g_alpha, dominated convergence in

    (1/x)sum_{n<=x}f_alpha(n)
       =sum_{d<=x}g_alpha(d)floor(x/d)/x

gives the positive mean

    c_alpha=product_p (1-1/p)
                   (1+(1-q)/(p(1-q/p))) > 0.

Positivity follows from positive local factors and summable deviations from 1, not an unproved prime-density hypothesis. In particular sum_{x/2<n<=x}f_alpha(n)=c_alpha*x/2+o_alpha(x).
