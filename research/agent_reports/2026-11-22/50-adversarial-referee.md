    ((U-mu)/sigma, (V-U)/sigma) => (N1, sqrt(2) N2).

Consequently (V-mu)/sigma has limiting variance 3, not 1. In particular the raw and frozen slopes have a common fluctuation, not two independent ones. This is a theorem for the specified sequential measure, not a probability postulate about primes.
