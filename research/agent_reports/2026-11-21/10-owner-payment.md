### G. Interpretation

The obstruction is finite-place cancellation **after** actual prime powers and translated partners have been combined, not the rank of a coefficient matrix. Logarithmic archimedean cancellation does not imply small algebraic degree; conversely, large degree does not imply a large real error. The geometric divisor theorem allows another field, growing normalization, inequalities instead of equalities, or a restricted nonlinear lift image. None is refuted by these examples.
