**JETCUT1 | A sufficient finite test at the actual operator interface.** Let $\mathcal O$ be a mixed-characteristic DVR, $K$ its fraction field, $\pi$ a uniformizer. In a finite integral chart $X_1,\ldots,X_d$ give each proposed native direction an integer label $r_i>1$; define $W(\alpha)=\prod_i r_i^{\alpha_i}$. In the prime-exponent application the labels are the actual high primes on one fixed low fibre. A physical choice of these directions has **not** been made. Write the invariant field of direction $i$ as $V_i=\sum_j A_{ij}(X)\partial_j$ with integral coefficients. For the [actual gauge formula](https://github.com/OVVO-Financial/RH_Lean/blob/e8c5fe628d9665921ec2e22f3aa46aeba1c163d2/RHLean/Geometry/NativeThetaJetEvaluation.lean#L126), let $g=N_{\rm lev}^2\,\mathrm{lineGauge}$ have zero constant term over $K$ and require $b_i=V_i g\in\mathcal O[[X]]$. For any integral germ $f$, constants of ordered derivatives of $e^g f$ equal constants of the same ordered products of $\nabla_i=V_i+b_i$ on $f$: $e^{-g}V_i(e^g f)=\nabla_i f$, $e^g(0)=1$. We do **not** need integrality of the entire exponential. This is a conditional simplification of a local input, not a choice of gauge for the host.

Fix $t\ge1$ and work modulo $\pi^t$. The following are equivalent for each $i$:

1. For every $j,\gamma$ with $W(\gamma)r_i<r_j$, the coefficient $[X^\gamma]A_{ij}$ vanishes modulo $\pi^t$.
2. For every real $T\ge0$, $\nabla_i$ sends germs supported on labels $>T$ into germs supported on labels $>T/r_i$ (after reduction).

For sufficiency, a derivative term in $\nabla_i X^\alpha$ has weight $W(\alpha)W(\gamma)/r_j$, hence at least $W(\alpha)/r_i$ unless its coefficient vanishes. Multiplication by $b_i$ never decreases weight. For necessity use $f=X_j$ and choose $r_iW(\gamma)\le T<r_j$; the multiplication term $b_i X_j$ has weight at least $r_j$ and cannot hide the offending coefficient. Formal sums pose no convergence issue: finitely many coordinates, all labels $>1$, and only finitely many terms at a bounded weight. **The coefficient test is finite** for each $i,j,t$. It is an equivalence with a first-order filtration law, **not** with RH or even with successful recovery on one subspace.

Iteration now proves that a row with label $h=\prod_i r_i^{a_i}$ cannot see any coefficient of weight $>h$ modulo $\pi^t$. Thus if the real chart, directions and integral germ maps are supplied and pass this test, lower-order cross terms cannot import an uncharged label from beyond that prefix. This is weaker than divisor-by-divisor locality: a smaller nondivisor label can still enter, and a truncated square block can still owe history. The test applies independently on each physical chart, not automatically to a dual chart, to an arithmetic subwindow, or to a changed prime partition. It does not prove the retained matrix is the unit matrix.
