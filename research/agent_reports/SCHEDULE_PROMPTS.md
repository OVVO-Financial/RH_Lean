Do not invent runs, schedules or results.
>
> Warm-start from verified evidence; invalidate only affected dependencies and reuse unchanged exact computations/counterexamples/CI. Pursue a genuinely independent mathematical fact, weaker quantified bound, decisive falsifier or hypothesis-discriminating experiment. Preserve original coefficients, weights, historical occurrences once, masks, clipping, boundaries, squareful/higher-factor mass, anchors, restoration and the complete native zero-target NNS covariance including every relevant cross term. Keep raw seats, eligible ancestry and admitted returned cells distinct. Audit both directions of suspected target equivalence. Do not infer that inequality from exact support extension, finite samples or a shrinking unproved exponent gap. An endpoint estimate needs separately proved full-prefix coverage. > Publish a small honest PARTIAL/IN_PROGRESS checkpoint in your assigned dated report after intake and before expensive work. Follow the current REPORT_TEMPLATE.md and README on agent-reports. Append meaningful results and a final capsule recording the independent fact learned, competing-hypothesis decision, actual bound/losses and smallest next decisive step. Reconcile authorized corrections without erasing valid prior evidence or failures. Use the current blob SHA; on conflict re-fetch/reconcile/retry once. Verify commit and readback. On failure say NOT_PUBLISHED and provide the report text. No credentials or confidential personal data in reports. No promised background results or fabricated CI.

> Respect live AGENTS permissions: the first six scheduled specialists write only their own dated report(s); only Lean Verification has its scoped agent/lean-verification/ proof-branch/draft-PR/Actions-only verification lane. No direct-main pushes, merges, auto-merge, other-report edits, policy/workflow-permission changes or schedule edits. A direct user-authorized maintenance task is separate from the scheduled research role. Actual broad connector access does not expand authorization.
