# Canonical-gap prefix Gram scan: the cancellation is between balanced and extreme sectors

This note records the first exact scan of the balanced/extreme decomposition from
`RHLean/Proof/BalancedCanonicalGap.lean`.

## Status

- **Exact finite computation:** yes. The segmented sieve uses integer Möbius values,
  exact largest-prime-factor recovery, integer canonical heights, and `__int128`
  energy accumulation.
- **Exact reconstruction check:** zero mismatches in every scan: the all-source
  balanced increment plus the all-source extreme increment equals the direct
  square-block Möbius increment at every block.
- **Analytic theorem:** still open. The scan decides which theorem should be attacked;
  it does not prove the uniform asymptotic estimate.

## Definitions tested

For a squarefree source `m`, let `q = P+(m)`, `c = m/q`,

```text
u = min(c,q),   v = max(c,q),   d = v-u,
h2 = d(u+v) = |q-c|(q+c) = 2Z.
```

A source is:

```text
balanced  when 0 < d < u,
extreme   when u <= d.
```

For a chosen high-band threshold `K_n`, let `b_n` and `e_n` be the signed Möbius
increments of the balanced and extreme high sectors in square block
`[n^2,(n+1)^2)`. For a window `[N,N+H)`, define local prefixes

```text
B_r = sum_{j=0}^r b_{N+j},
E_r = sum_{j=0}^r e_{N+j}.
```

The exact ledger is

```text
Q_BB  = sum_r B_r^2,
Q_EE  = sum_r E_r^2,
Q_BE  = sum_r B_r E_r,
Q_tot = Q_BB + 2 Q_BE + Q_EE = sum_r (B_r+E_r)^2.
```

Thresholds in the scanner are expressed using doubled height:

```text
all:       h2 > 0,
Lambda=L:  h2 > 2 L n,
delta=a:   h2 > 2 n floor(n^a).
```

So the anti-alignment is not generated by the already-controlled low-imbalance band.
It persists in the genuinely high canonical-height population.

## What the scan decides

The remaining theorem must **not** be split into independent balanced and extreme
energy estimates. On the tested RH-scale windows, both diagonal energies are enormous;
the small quantity is the coupled form

```text
Q_BB + 2 Q_BE + Q_EE.
```

The correct frozen analytic target is therefore

```text
sum_{r=0}^{H-1} |B_r + E_r|^2  <<_eps  H N^(2+eps),
```

with the balanced/extreme cross term retained. A theorem proving separate bounds for
`Q_BB` and `Q_EE` would be far stronger than the observed mechanism and attacks the
wrong objects.

The cancellation can be separated into two exact defects. Writing

```text
rho = Q_BE / sqrt(Q_BB Q_EE),
```

we have

```text
Q_tot
 = (sqrt(Q_BB) - sqrt(Q_EE))^2
   + 2 sqrt(Q_BB Q_EE) (1 + rho).
```

Thus the remaining proof has two coupled obligations:

1. **norm matching:** the balanced and extreme prefix energies have nearly equal size;
2. **anti-alignment:** their prefix vectors have `rho` close to `-1`.

The scan shows both effects, with anti-alignment carrying most of the cancellation on
long square-scale windows.

## Formal companion

`RHLean/Proof/CanonicalGapPrefixGram.lean` formalizes, without analytic assumptions:

- additivity of finite prefix sums;
- the exact energy ledger `Q_tot = Q_BB + 2 Q_BE + Q_EE`;
- the prefix Gram kernel
  `K_H(i,j) = H - max(i,j)`;
- the arithmetic balanced/extreme instantiation.

## Reproduction

```bash
gcc -O3 -march=native -Wall -Wextra \
  -o canonical_gap_prefix_scan canonical_gap_prefix_scan.c -lm

./canonical_gap_prefix_scan 1000 1000
./canonical_gap_prefix_scan 5000 5000
./canonical_gap_prefix_scan 10000 10000
./canonical_gap_prefix_scan 50000 1000
./canonical_gap_prefix_scan 100000 1000
```

The scanner exits nonzero if the all-source balanced/extreme reconstruction fails in
any square block.
