## What the independent investigation narrows

### First-bad is a historical-wall predicate, not a local deficit

For a wall `K R log R`, write `s_R=R log R`, `D_R=pi(R²)-VF_can(R²)`, `L_R(K)=D_R+K s_R`. Then exactly
```text
L_(R+1)(K)-L_R(K) = P_R-V_R+K(s_(R+1)-s_R).
```
A lower crossing at `R+1` requires `V_R-P_R > K(s_(R+1)-s_R)`, but this is only a **necessary local test**, not a global first-bad theorem.

A diagnostic genuine-prime lower breach with `K=0.019` occurs near `R=1031` when the safe history is **restarted at R=1000**; this does NOT necessarily satisfy the formal global first-bad predicate, which demands safety at every `S>=2`. At block `R=1030`, reported values are `P_R=134`, `V_R≈148.534128`, `s_(R+1)-s_R≈7.937799`; the local slope threshold `(V_R-P_R)/(s_(R+1)-s_R)≈1.831`, less than `K=2`. This one block CANNOT force a local `K=2` lower crossing.

### First-bad normalized budget is conditional and uses raw weights

From `VF_MID_FIRST_BAD_NORMALIZED_PARTIAL_MOMENT.lean`:

M_R = |D_R^raw| + sum_(odd genuine candidate seats) |vfMidOddSignedSeatCharge(R,n)|
