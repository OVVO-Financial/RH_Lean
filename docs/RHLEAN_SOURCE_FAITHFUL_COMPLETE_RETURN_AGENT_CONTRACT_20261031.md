## 4. Legitimate signed closures

Write the signed recombination before taking norms. For real streams P and C on the actual root range:

```text
||P+C||_2^2 = ||P||_2^2 + ||C||_2^2 + 2 <P,C>.
```

For complex streams use the real part of the inner product. Four legitimate routes remain available:

1. Aggregate small-remainder bounds: every remaining contribution has enough power saving and ordinary Cauchy-Schwarz preserves it.
2. Internally completed signed packets: large compensating partners are combined before estimation, with exact residual and coverage.
3. One-sided covariance or defect bounds: control the required signed sum without imposing an unnecessary absolute bound on its negative part.
4. Independently proved complete-source absorption or multiscale recurrence: a strict contraction closes after every return cost is included.

Do not infer a power saving from a favorable sign or one fixed-factor reduction at one scale. If one summand is already much smaller than another in L2, the reverse triangle inequality prevents it from canceling the latter's dominant size; seek compensation inside the remaining source or regroup the large partners.

Do not demand an arbitrary-vector operator norm when the consumer only needs an arithmetic quadratic-form estimate on genuine coefficients. Conversely, do not replace the genuine vector with arbitrary signs and keep calling the resulting model source-faithful. Never spend the same negative square, compensation credit or boundary cancellation twice in different inequalities.

At the shell level, the repository's exact Green-Kubo decomposition gives E(H)=D(H)+2V(H), with D(H)<=3H^2. For a target eta in (0,1/2), a one-sided upper bound on 2V(H) at H^(5/2-eta+eps) is sufficient. Componentwise nonpositivity and an absolute-value covariance bound are not required. That covariance estimate remains an open arithmetic input, not a theorem supplied by this paragraph.

## 5. Uniformity, coverage and the net power budget

A fixed gain must have the order of quantifiers:

```text
exists eta > 0, forall eps > 0, exists C_eps and H_eps,
forall allowed scale H >= H_eps and required auxiliary parameters:
    complete_quantity <= C_eps * H^(baseline - eta + eps).
```

Parameters may be deliberately chosen as functions of H when the proof permits that choice, but prove their admissibility and all resulting costs. Do not silently replace uniformity with a favorable subsequence. State which quantities are fixed before H/eps and which vary. Constants must be independent of every parameter being quantified uniformly.

Fixed-seed O_s estimates are not automatically uniform for s growing with H. Track the seed dependence, the number of seed classes, omega(s), smooth/rough boundaries, partial periods and resonant exceptions. A polylogarithmic factor for a fixed seed need not remain polylogarithmic over a growing seed family. Do not assume the generalization of a paper identity is already formalized.

For K packets, the safe inequality is:

```text
sum_R |sum_lambda P_(lambda,R)|^2
    <= K * sum_lambda sum_R |P_(lambda,R)|^2.
```

A per-packet H^(p-gamma) bound can therefore incur K^2 in total; an already aggregated sum-of-packet-energies bound incurs only the additional K shown above. Prove a cheaper signed aggregation when necessary. Disjoint arithmetic occurrences do not establish root-average orthogonality.

Treat exceptional sets quantitatively. With only |S_R|=O(H), N_bad roots cost O(N_bad*H^2); a density-one statement alone does not fit the square-shell target. Either prove sufficient sparsity or estimate the exceptional source more sharply. Complete-period estimates require an incomplete-window theorem at the actual range used.

After transforming to the SAME consumer normalization, if the contribution bounds are H^(p-gamma_j+loss_j+eps), a sufficient net margin is:

```text
eta_net <= min_j (gamma_j - loss_j), with eta_net > 0.
```

Take the worst surviving exponent, not a sum of unrelated local savings. Include polynomial packet-count and transfer losses; absorb only fixed logarithmic/subpower factors through a proved epsilon budget. Fix epsilon small enough to retain a strictly positive margin. UNKNOWN is mandatory if any binding bill remains unproved.

An independently proved recurrence for a nonnegative correctly normalized complete quantity,

```text
F(2H) <= theta*F(H) + C*H^(-delta),  0 <= theta < 1, delta > 0,
```

with controlled initial scale yields a power saving along dyadic scales for every 0<eta<min(delta,-log_2 theta) when theta>0; theta=0 is immediate. Prove any needed intermediate-scale transfer. A constant contraction becomes an exponent gain only through a valid repeated complete-source recurrence. A local diagonal/child contraction is not that recurrence.

Likewise, a proved E<=theta*E+C*H^(p-gamma), theta<1, legitimately closes by absorption. It is not circular merely because E occurs twice; the independent derivation of the strict inequality is what must be audited.


```text

Remainder(H) = sum_(H<=R<2H) [c_R^2 + 2*t_R*c_R].
```

