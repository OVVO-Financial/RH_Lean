## 1. What qualifies as a clear continuation

Before selecting substantive work, record the following for the chosen route.

1. **Original source and state.** Specify the actual signed expression, masks, cutoffs, normalization, source SHA and all uniformity. Define the statement P(b) being improved. For a Mertens route, distinguish an exact fixed-power bound from the every-positive-loss statement. For a prime, Nyman, envelope or Hecke route, give the proved transfer to the intended endpoint without falsely identifying carriers.
2. **Complete map, closed improvement, or direct terminal consumer.** State the proposed implication P(b) → P(Φ(b)), its full interval of validity, all losses and all complementary return terms. Give the exact initial certified input. A closed self-improvement at every possible actual supremum b>1/2 also qualifies: specify the theorem that supplies P(b) at that supremum and the strict contradiction it would yield. Alternatively state a direct source theorem and its exact consumer at 1/2, identifying a substantive new arithmetic mechanism for proving it.
3. **Renewal and endpoint.** For an iterative route, show why each output supplies the next input and prove convergence to 1/2. For a closed improvement, justify substitution at the actual epsilon-closed supremum. No zero need attain that supremum, and no positive gain uniform as b tends to 1/2 is required; retain the fixed-b family-uniformity condition below. Include every parameter, analytic domain, height, conductor, slot, cutoff and coefficient-mass gate. A strict-input-only premise (Theta<b) cannot be applied at b=Theta: it needs a proved convergence, local-uniformity or other endpoint argument. A positive gain at the initial b, or a map with an unknown limiting barrier, does not qualify.
4. **First new arithmetic step.** Separate already-proved interfaces from the first unproved estimate. Explain the independently derived arithmetic information intended to prove that estimate, and give the first checkable falsifier. Renaming the desired endpoint as a “supplier,” inserting a full-response bound into its own construction, or assuming a target-equivalent majorant is not a new mechanism.
5. **Honest status and downstream use.** Label each edge PROVED_IN_LEAN, ACCEPT_ON_PAPER, SELF_AUDIT, OPEN, CONDITIONAL, FINITE_COMPUTATION or REFUTED, as appropriate. Name the exact later step that consumes the present work. An explicit open research mechanism can qualify for attack; an unproved premise must remain unproved in every report and theorem.

A direct proof route need not imitate an exponent iteration. A new derivation of an RH-equivalent source bound is legitimate mathematics when its actual mechanism and first independent step are specified. Merely displaying the equivalent bound is not a bootstrap.

Constants may depend on the fixed stage, target epsilon and fixed source parameters. They must precede the growing arithmetic scale. To establish every b>1/2 by a finite number of iterations, one does **not** need a single constant uniform over infinitely many iterations. Conversely, constants or sources chosen after the individual scale do not supply an all-scale theorem. Record how every positive loss is spent.

For a common supremum over an infinite character family, distinguish constants from exponents: constants may depend on the individual character, but a claimed strict improvement of the common supremum needs a positive exponent gain uniform over that family at each fixed input boundary, or another explicit extremal argument. Per-character gains tending to zero do not lower the family supremum. No uniform positive gain as the boundary itself tends to 1/2 is required. A native zeta consumer does not by itself establish an all-Dirichlet or angular-family conclusion.
