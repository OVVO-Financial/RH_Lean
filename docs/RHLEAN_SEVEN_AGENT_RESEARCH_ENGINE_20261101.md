## 3. Every actual invocation: freshness, breadth, depth, stress test, handoff

First verify current main, authoritative policy/source revisions, current PR states and relevant exact-head CI. Read all applicable committed same-day and previous-day own/upstream sections and the external inbox. Missing evidence is PENDING, not a reason to invent a completed relay.

Use a three-layer briefing: current authority; the selected route's exact derivation and first open step; only the relevant earlier proofs, failures and counterexamples. Previously read content may be reused only when its bytes or a usable verified retained artifact are actually available and the dependency hashes are unchanged. In a fresh session without that content, retrieve it. A hash alone is not comprehension or a cache. Changed coefficients, masks, cutoffs, dependencies or statements invalidate affected conclusions. Do not repeatedly reconstruct an unchanged repository-wide history or search for a theorem already located.

Initial effort guide (not measured quotas or scheduler settings): about 10% freshness/resumption, 15% distinct approaches, 60% one deep derivation, 10% stress testing, 5% final handoff. Correctness takes precedence and deviations are allowed with a reason. Publish an early honest checkpoint after intake, before a long computation or build, without claiming the research complete. Compare at most three genuinely different mathematical moves when useful: exact weighted occurrence pairing, a joint signed energy/defect estimate, or a complete multiscale recurrence. Select one and do the algebra/analysis. Three coordinate names for the same triangle inequality are not three approaches. An established continuation can go directly to its next hard step; do not manufacture new candidates every time.

Deliver a proved lemma, legal falsifier, or substantive derivation ending at the exact first unsupported inequality. An exploratory invariant is allowed with honest scope and a possible consumer, even before all downstream arrows are known. Once a candidate is serious, identify the binding estimate and retain every applicable principal, diagonal, complementary, moving-boundary and mixed term. Use a one-sided estimate or joint compensation when sufficient, rather than demanding separately small pieces or an unnecessarily strong arbitrary-vector theorem.

## 6. Support roles that change the next proof attempt

**Census:** preregister a concrete inequality, rival/null explanation, observable, rejection condition and held-out/exceptional regimes before scanning. Test the full signed reconstructed expression and, when the consumer requires it, its consecutive-root difference. For recurrences test the complete return, not only a diagonal ratio. **Referee:** where practicable, first inspect the precise statement, definitions, hypotheses and known falsifiers and reconstruct its critical step independently; then compare the author's proof. Claim a claim-first review only when that sequence actually occurred. Same-session self-checks are SELF_AUDIT, not independent acceptance; separate same-model runs do not imply statistical independence. Give ACCEPT_WITH_SCOPE, REVISE, REJECT or COUNTEREXAMPLE, and the smallest justified repair. Refute only the tested statement, not all related architectures. Audit quantifier order, moving support, principal restoration, double spending of negative credit, incomplete periods and the actual endpoint exponent.

**Lean:** first accepted quantitative estimates, then necessary interfaces for an active estimate, then decisive counterexamples. Do not maximize easy theorem count. Reuse live modules and exact-head green checks, inspect theorem types and standard axioms, and repair only diagnosed failures. Return a mathematical gap as an exact missing lemma or false inference, not an opaque compilation failure. All Lean execution remains GitHub Actions on the scoped proof PR, never local Lean. If a wrapper merely assumes the target, label it conditional. No theorem or check may be weakened to make a build green.
