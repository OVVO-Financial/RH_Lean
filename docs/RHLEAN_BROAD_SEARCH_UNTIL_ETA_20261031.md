## 2. What counts as the positive-eta gate

The gate concerns a new, uniform improvement of the relevant COMPLETE endpoint, not merely a positive parameter somewhere upstream. A candidate must establish all of the following:

1. **Fixed positive gain.** A single eta > 0 is chosen before the asymptotic scale and epsilon. It is independent of H, X, the endpoint, parent, modulus or family being quantified. No lower numerical size is required. A rigorously proved existential positive constant is sufficient; an explicit rational witness is preferred when available, but is not an additional mathematical prerequisite.
2. **Original arithmetic source and uniformity.** State the actual primes, Mobius coefficients, ideals, weights, admission boundaries, parameter ranges and complete quantifier order. The estimate must hold on its required all-scale range, not just a finite census, favorable subsequence, completed window, frozen coefficient or chosen parent.
3. **Complete return and consumer.** Prove the occurrence-preserving bridge and account for every relevant complement, principal mode, diagonal, mixed term, exceptional/resonant class, activation/return term, tail, smoothing/grid/height cost and interpolation step. Record the net endpoint exponent after these costs; no target-strength supplier may remain assumed.
4. **Genuinely new arithmetic.** The proved incoming 7/8 bound may be used as an input and guidepost. Identify the additional independently proved restriction that makes the OUTPUT strictly stronger. Re-expressing the imported baseline, improving a nonbinding constant or matching component exponents does not suffice.
5. **Independent and formal verification.** Obtain an independent Referee acceptance at the exact stated scope, then Lean verification of the gain and its complete consumer at an identified head with successful relevant CI, exact theorem-type inspection and the standard-axiom audit. Reviewed paper proofs remain valuable candidates during verification; they do not suspend broad coverage. An unmerged verified proof must be labeled PR-head evidence, never main evidence.

For example, a complete sharp-square-shell theorem can use

    exists eta > 0, forall eps > 0, exists C H0,
      forall integer H >= H0,
        sum_(H <= R < 2H) |Delta_R|^2 <= C * H^(5/2-eta+eps),
    Delta_R = sum_(R^2 <= n < (R+1)^2) mu(n).

It qualifies only with the verified source identification and full interpolation giving Mertens exponent 7/8-eta/4+eps. The positive margin must survive the choice of epsilon. No fixed decimal threshold for eta is imposed.

This shell theorem is an EXAMPLE, not a mandatory common target. A complete VF/SAT/JOH, CODIV, FINITE-AC, CORR4, MU-LIFT, OAI-NNS or NYMAN argument with its own verified strict endpoint improvement is equally eligible. A stronger direct RH-scale result also qualifies without being forced through square-clock notation. Preserve zeta-only versus all-Dirichlet scope; neither substitutes for the other.

**Insufficient to pass the gate:** a local reciprocal tau > 0, weighted diagonal contraction, isolated-sector power saving, paid first-band wedge, complete-period average, coefficient identity, green algebra PR, finite fitted exponent, changed constant, conditional eta hypothesis, or a partial estimate with unbounded return/complement. These are genuine intermediate results when proved; they are not permission to narrow the portfolio.

Gate labels:

- SEARCH_BROAD: no verified complete endpoint gain; this is the default.
- CANDIDATE_ETA: a proposed or paper-proved positive gain is under review/formalization; keep broad allocation unchanged.
- VERIFIED_ETA: all five requirements have documented evidence. The Coordinator may recommend increased concentration to the human owner; it is not an automatic schedule, permission or merge change.

No agent may silently set VERIFIED_ETA from a report headline. If an alleged gain fails source, uniformity or return review, retain the useful narrower lemma and keep SEARCH_BROAD.
