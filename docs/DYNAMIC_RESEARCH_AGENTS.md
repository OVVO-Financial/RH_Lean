# RH_Lean — Dynamic seven-agent mathematical research protocol

**Adopted as proposed research governance, 2026-10-14.** This document is subordinate to [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) (agent authority, conduct and permissions) and [CURRENT_PROOF_CONTRACT.md](../CURRENT_PROOF_CONTRACT.md) (current canonical mathematical target). Original compiled Lean theorems establish what has been proved. A dated agent report is research evidence, not proof authority. This policy changes **how** the seven agents investigate, not the mathematical target or existing formal code. No RH claim is made.

## Why the research cycle changes

The prior specialized research loop excelled at exact local ledgers, wheels, asymptotics, refutations and Lean infrastructure. But directing every specialty at one prescribed signed-collision/payment hypothesis risks repeatedly rediscovering the same missing global bound. Research must include genuinely different mathematical *mechanisms*, independent new inputs and early adversarial tests. The Coordinator is a mathematical theory architect, not a report aggregator.

## Daily scientific method

1. **Refresh facts and authority:** read live main HEAD, `AGENTS.md` and `CURRENT_PROOF_CONTRACT.md`, original relevant Lean source, current PR/CI; read both today and preceding calendar day's actual dated stage reports on `agent-reports` and new `EXTERNAL_HANDOFFS.md` entries. Evidence from yesterday is `YESTERDAY=CARRY_FORWARD`, not today's completed stage.
2. **Locate the independent mathematical gap:** exact identity, input to be bounded, original carrier, consumer statement, and both directions of suspected target equivalence. Do not mistake a conditional theorem or new naming convention for new mathematics.
3. **Compare mechanisms:** maintain roughly 2–3 *truly different* candidate routes when warranted. Suggested families: signed arithmetic invariant; an inverse forbidden configuration theorem; a joint nonlocal kernel/covariance theorem. Finite A/C and CORR-4 are possible distinct alternatives. No route quota is mandatory.
4. **Attack rather than merely extend:** each research agent should attempt a substantive original hypothesis, mathematical proof, counterexample or corrective obstruction when tools permit. Prefer decisive falsification to a large new unmotivated finite census.
5. **Request complementary expertise:** Architect assigns exact falsifiable questions, but other agents may originate competing mechanisms. Specialist reports must state why the new mathematics changes proof prospects.
6. **Adversarial acceptance:** referee checks original exact coefficients, quantifiers, forward/reverse equivalences, complete covariance and biggest honest counterexample. Correct local theorems deserve credit without artificial claims of global closure.
7. **Formalize accepted results:** only independently proved mathematics should become Lean code; Agent #7 owns the authorized proof-branch/draft-PR/Actions CI/repair workflow. No new axioms, proof holes or weakened validation. The human owner alone merges.
8. **Preserve research:** publish early verified `IN_PROGRESS` stage checkpoint, substantive findings and a final timestamped capsule on the assigned `agent-reports` path. One automatic scheduled run/agent/day is the current user-stated budget; a GitHub report does not trigger other agents or promise background work.

## Mechanism card — required for strategic evaluation

- **Name and source:** stable claim ID, proposing agent, exact main/source SHAs.
- **Original mathematical carrier:** genuine primes or Möbius coefficients, occurrence identities, original weights and signs, multiplicities, chronology, masks, clips, historical anchor, squareful and restoration terms, relevant full signed cross-family interactions.
- **Exact candidate theorem:** all quantified variables, range, constants, uniformity and sign/error direction.
- **Independent mathematical insight:** a genuinely new arithmetic law, inverse implication, transform with a new provable property, or external theorem with its verified hypotheses.
- **Implication:** exact proved/conditional arrows to the native original source and canonical endpoint or a legitimate sufficient alternative; identify every missing arrow.
- **Counterexample and falsifier:** strongest tested witness and smallest decisive next computation or proof; distinguish executed from proposed tests.
- **Evidence state:** `CONJECTURE` / `FINITE_COMPUTATION` / `IDENTITY_ONLY` / `PAPER_PROOF` / `CONDITIONAL` / `REFUTED` / `TARGET_EQUIVALENT` / `PROVED_IN_LEAN` with exact formal evidence when claimed.
- **Portfolio decision:** CONTINUE / REVISE / SUSPEND / REJECT; next responsible agent and decisive question.

## Mathematical acceptance and no-go screen

1. **Authentic physical source first.** For an estimate on the genuine prime source, preserve original signs, occurrence multiplicity, actual prime owner, native weights, chronological support, masks/clips, squareful and exceptional terms, anchor, compensation and all diagonal/off-diagonal/cross-family terms. One historical prime cannot be paid multiple times by cloned descendants.
2. **Global covariance is not a sum of favorable local squares.** A selected packet sign, cap-four wheel theorem or isolated negative NNS pair cannot be aggregated without the exact omitted quadratic residual.
3. **Model is not primes.** Floor-Li differs from Li by construction; its event atoms are not genuine primes. The Li-density absolute Poisson collision budget is finite, but the naive actual-prime indicator replacement has divergent reciprocal-prime mass. A centered actual-prime *sitewise* hard-core correction has a coherent negative logarithmic-logarithmic mode at paper-proof level, refuting that scalar transfer only. A divergent auxiliary loss need not exceed the permissible final endpoint error.
4. **A transport identity is not a bound.** Exact Abel/Duhamel, factor reindexing and source-to-boundary formulas require an independently established size estimate.
5. **Check both directions to target.** Several reported 'smaller last lemmas' are equivalent to the canonical statement according to compiled audits; a true new mechanism needs an independent arithmetic/analytic input.
6. **No unproved uniformity.** Expose dependence on growing cutoffs, constants, factor classes, discarded correlations, summation/rearrangement and recurrences. Finite computation is evidence, not an infinite theorem.
7. **No blanket pessimism.** A rigorous no-go theorem must name the technique or proposed statement it excludes, not reject all possible signed or renormalized approaches; preserve the strongest true corrected lemma.

## Reporting and implementation boundaries

- Seven standing report stages live on branch `agent-reports` under `research/agent_reports/YYYY-MM-DD/` with their existing filenames; Coordinator AM/PM are historical *logical* phases, not two automatic runs.
- Today and previous-day handoffs plus external direct-session research must be read at each actual invocation; missing stages remain `PENDING`. Reuse source-valid past proof/experiment work; invalidate only changed dependencies.
- Six research roles are report-only within their assigned stage file. Lean Verification alone may write its scoped `agent/lean-verification/` proof branches and draft PRs, compile using real GitHub Actions, fix actual failures, and hand off to a human for merge.
- Keep mathematical status, PR/CI status and reporting status separate. `CI_GREEN` for a conditional statement does not prove its antecedent; an unmerged theorem is not present in `main`. A review cannot impersonate a Lean build.
- Do not change protected sources from a scheduled agent, push or merge directly into main, introduce `sorry`/`admit`/unproved axioms, invent tool outputs, or modify schedules/permissions via prose.
- To change the strategy or canonical objective, propose reviewed updates to the live central Markdown files. Saved Studio instructions and schedules require separate authorized edits.

**Daily opening question for the Coordinator and all researchers:** *What newly discovered mathematical fact changes how we should attack the missing global bound?* A valid conclusion of `NO_NEW_RESULT` is preferable to a manufactured breakthrough.
