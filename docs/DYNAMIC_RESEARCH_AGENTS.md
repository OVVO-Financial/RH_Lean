## Mechanism card: required before substantial expenditure

Each live candidate has a stable ID and:

1. **Carrier and source:** current main and relevant blob SHAs; genuine coefficients; physical occurrences; endpoint convention; weights, multiplicity, masks/clips, anchor and restoration. Identify whether the object is raw seats, eligible ancestry, admitted returned cells or actual Mobius coefficients.
2. **Claim with quantifiers:** variables, domains, constants, dependence on growing parameters, all-scale or finite scope, and the exact inequality direction.
3. **Independent input:** the arithmetic theorem to prove, distinct from the desired final estimate. An identity is allowed as structural work but must not masquerade as that estimate.
4. **Consumer and logical strength:** exact source bridge; all missing arrows; comparison with canonical VF or a legitimate alternative; compiled forward AND reverse implications; stronger-than-needed assumptions explicitly marked.
5. **Competing prediction:** at least one plausible rival or null explanation and a finite observable on which the predictions differ. No forced numerical experiment where a proof/falsifier already decides the issue.
6. **Falsification and status:** strongest valid regression, proposed versus executed tests, evidence state and current result. Explain any exemption because the witness does not satisfy the proposal's actual carrier hypotheses.
7. **Decision:** CONTINUE / REVISE / SUSPEND / REJECT, the next independent question and responsible specialty. Multiple cards with the same unpaid input are one lane, not diversity.

A useful early result can be a weaker bound, sign restriction, exact transport defect or impossibility result. State its actual strength and why it changes the next question. Do not demand the full RH-scale bound from every small lemma; do not award RH-scale credit to a lemma that does not imply it.

## Adversarial decision rules

- **No-narrowing:** search both directions. An endpoint-equivalent mismatch or universal amplitude-preservation condition is not a known independent input.
- **No source substitution:** eligible FTA ancestry, raw seats and masked/clipped cells are different. A theorem/refutation is carried only as far as its proved bridge.
- **No finite-to-infinite leap:** a positive finite prefix, extension guard or observed one-transition pattern is not automatically permanent.
- **No omitted coherent mode:** retain same-tag and cross-depth interactions, including the paper sitewise correction's negative logarithmic-logarithmic term whenever that model is used.
- **No indiscriminate pessimism:** a fixed-capacity obstruction excludes that covering method, not variable-capacity signed reassembly; a scalar Li no-go does not refute every nonlocal response theorem.
- **Repair preference:** after falsification, preserve the exact useful lemma and formulate its strongest true scoped replacement. Apply the mandatory regressions in the brief or explain their inapplicability.
