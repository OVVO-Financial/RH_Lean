## 1. Concrete finite principle and authentic construction

Start with GlobalTheory.DescentFamily.descent. In the source's slope_sq convention, let A and B be finitely generated integral Hermitian lattices over a totally complex number field, with genuine Gram norm structures and A nonzero. Construct linear maps phi_h for h in Fin q such that:

- Every phi_h maps A into B.
- Their collective map is injective.
- At every embedding sigma and every vector v,
  \[
  \sum_h \|\phi_h(v)\|_{B,\sigma}^2
  \le e^{2P}\|v\|_{A,\sigma}^2.
  \]
- B is spanned over the number field by elements of B of norm at most exp(Lambda) at every embedding.
- Lambda and epsilon_rk are nonnegative, and the actual ranks satisfy
  \[
  q\,\operatorname{rk}(B)\le(1+\epsilon_{\rm rk})\operatorname{rk}(A).
  \]

The finite theorem concludes
\[
t\le\operatorname{slope\_sq}(A)-2P
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{slope\_sq}(B)
\ge t-\epsilon_{\rm rk}(|t|+2\Lambda).
\]

Extract or simplify the proof through the joint injection A into B^q, its map-height bound and the rank defect. Preserve the squared-norm slope normalization and all factors of two. This is a finite lattice-slope argument; it does not impose an unrelated decreasing numerical recurrence or base case.

Then construct its inputs. The inspected mapsCore proof constructs q=|S| maps from an integral section and level translations. It requires a non-zero-divisor determinant and pays the combined norm cost |S|^3 B^2 <= exp(2b). The actual covering supplier constructs the short section and bounds
\[
|S|^3e^{2Cz^9}\le e^{135z^2+2Cz^9}\le e^{2b}
\]
under its size regime. Rank growth is supplied using jet order, weight gaps, valuation/multiplicity, component degrees and shares. These are source mechanisms to reconstruct, not premises to leave uninstantiated.

In the lower-slope application,
\[
P=b,\qquad \Lambda=PP\,\kappa,\qquad
\epsilon_{\rm rk}=b/(PP\,\kappa),
\]
with PP*kappa>0 supplied by the original size regime, so 2 epsilon_rk Lambda = 2b. Keep height and rank-defect costs together. Carry the resulting lower slope, denominator/integrality controls and bad-subspace estimate through to the actual normalized covolume. A freely chosen determinant, rank count or normalized covolume does not supply this witness.
